Reading order

Four vertical columns, read from right to left. Punctuation is omitted from the artwork.

What this shows

Every partial mark is deposited by the installed calligraphy engine from ordered, frozen stroke programs. The still and film share the same frame renderer.

Source boundary

The programs were fitted from individually normalized static template outlines and medians. They do not preserve an artist’s hand, historical brush motion, pressure, or timing.
